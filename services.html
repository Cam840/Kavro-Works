Before we get started

A few
good questions.

What should I text you?

A photo of the item and where it will go, your city, and a product link if you have one. For multiple items, send the whole list.

Do I need the item and hardware ready?

Tell me what you already have when you text. We’ll confirm the item, mounting hardware, and any additional materials before scheduling.

Can you handle more than one job in a visit?

Yes—send your complete list so I can plan the time and confirm the total price together.

How do I book a time?

Call or text me. We’ll agree on the work, price, and appointment directly. Availability depends on the job and location.
